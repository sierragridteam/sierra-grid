# Following the evidence

Selected concept: **Question trail**. Start with a question about this place; a live answer opens more specific questions. Follow a forecast into its hourly evidence, a road interruption into its source record and revisions, a burn answer into the original phone transcript, or a radio into its reported measurements. A persistent trail marks the route taken. The compass and full collection index allow immediate access at any point; this is neither a forced wizard nor a simulated chatbot.

Live observations determine the follow-up evidence offered. Editorial prompts are questions, not invented accounts of conditions. Every answer keeps scope, source, time and uncertainty visible. The regional rollup does not substitute for condition-level health. Current snapshots, revisions and measurements remain distinct.

Visual direction: warm paper, dark forest ink, vermilion trail annotations, oversized Newsreader questions, ruled evidence strips and a compact compass. Shared Sierra typography, logo, controls and time-series primitives anchor the product. No map or node list determines its structure.

## Considered alternatives

1. Fieldbook: six editorial chapters with selected live observations and annotated evidence. Strong breadth and legibility; less interaction-original.
2. Evidence loom: juxtapose two domain timelines to explore contemporaneous observations without implying causation. Distinct interaction, but alignment of unlike clocks adds substantial interpretation risk.
3. Question trail (selected by coordinator): sequential, branching inquiry with direct evidence access and persistent context.
